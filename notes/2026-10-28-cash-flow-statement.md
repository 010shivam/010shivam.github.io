[Sample text, replace with your own.] When I open a company's annual report, I go to the cash flow statement before the income statement. Profit depends on judgment calls about when revenue is recognised and how costs are spread. Cash either arrived or it did not.

## What I check

First, whether operating cash flow tracks net income over several years. A persistent gap, with profit rising and cash lagging, is a prompt to read the notes on receivables and inventory.

Second, how much of free cash flow is consumed by capital expenditure and by buybacks.

## What I am still learning

Working-capital swings can be seasonal or structural, and telling them apart takes industry knowledge I am still building.

[Sample text, replace with your own.] In a basic discounted cash flow model, a lower discount rate raises the present value of every future cash flow, and it raises long-dated cash flows the most. That is why growth stocks tend to react more strongly to rate news.

## Where the argument breaks

Central banks usually cut because growth is weakening. If earnings expectations fall by more than the discount rate does, multiples can still contract.
